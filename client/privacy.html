Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

  • Discord account information (username, email, avatar) via OAuth
  • Roblox account credentials (User ID, API Key) that you provide
  • Audio conversion history and upload preferences
  • Payment information processed securely through Midtrans

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account via Discord OAuth
  • To convert and upload audio files to your Roblox account
  • To process subscription payments
  • To provide customer support

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using encrypted database connections, AES-256 for credentials, Cloudflare R2 for files, and HTTPS.

Third-Party Services

Discord, Roblox, Midtrans, MongoDB Atlas, Cloudflare.

Your Rights

Access, delete, unlink Roblox accounts, cancel subscriptions, export history.
